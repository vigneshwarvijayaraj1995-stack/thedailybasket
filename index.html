Our Story

Established in 2026, The Daily Basket was born out of a simple desire: to make premium, boutique pantry staples and high-end household care products accessible to the local community in Toronto. We believe that the items you use every day—from the oil you cook with to the soap you use—should bring a touch of luxury and quality to your routine.


We focus exclusively on long-shelf-life goods, partnering directly with incredible producers who prioritize quality, ethical sourcing, and exceptional craftsmanship over mass production.
